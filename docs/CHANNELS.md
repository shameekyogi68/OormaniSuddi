| after each post | area digests | WhatsApp community | `whatsapp_<group>.txt`, 3 stories at most |
| as it happens | real footage | YouTube (+ Shorts) | the footage skills only — never AI card reels (AGENTS rule 9) |

ಕುಂದಾಪುರ–ಬೈಂದೂರು · ಉಡುಪಿ–ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಾವರ–ಕಾರ್ಕಳ · ಮಂಗಳೂರು. Each render
writes one post per group. Two or three items a day, never a flood.

**Facebook groups.** Keep a list of the 10–15 most active local groups.
Post a ಮುಖ್ಯ ಸುದ್ದಿ there using `facebook_group_<k>.txt` (question first,
no outside link), and no more than once a week to any one group.

**YouTube Shorts** from real footage: title in Kannada, then `|`, then the
place and topic in English. The first three description lines carry what
happened and the towns.
