# Advertising and sponsorship policy — ಊರ್ಮನಿ ಸುದ್ದಿ (D118)

Public, on purpose: readers are entitled to know how a news channel is paid,
and advertisers are entitled to know the terms before they pay. The rules
here are enforced in code (`brand/business.py :: check_deal`) and by the
`ad-standards` desk; none can be waived.

## 1 · Money buys advertising, never news

* A sponsor buys one of these, labelled on the work itself:

  | Product | Label |
  |---|---|
  | sponsored festival greeting | ಪ್ರಾಯೋಜಿತ |
  | ad post or reel | ಜಾಹೀರಾತು |
  | sponsored video (YouTube) | ಪ್ರಾಯೋಜಿತ |
  | paid coverage of an organiser's event | ಪ್ರಾಯೋಜಿತ |
  | paid live stream | ಪ್ರಾಯೋಜಿತ |
  | personal notice (ಶ್ರದ್ಧಾಂಜಲಿ, ಅಭಿನಂದನೆ, ಶುಭಾಶಯ) | ಜಾಹೀರಾತು |
  | video production for the client, not published by us | — |

* **Not for sale, at any price:** a news story; a place in ಸ್ಪೀಡ್ ನ್ಯೂಸ್,
  ಸುದ್ದಿ ಸಾರ or ಮುಖ್ಯ ಸುದ್ದಿ; how a story is written; whether a story runs;
  the removal or softening of anything published. Corrections follow
  [`CORRECTIONS.md`](CORRECTIONS.md) and nothing else.
* An organiser who pays for event coverage gets a labelled film of their
  event. Whether the event is also news is the newsroom's decision, made as
  for any other event, and that report is never paid for.
* Paid work never wears the news design — no red news kicker, no ಬ್ರೇಕಿಂಗ್,
  no news masthead — and never a news word: it is not a ವರದಿ, a ವಿಶೇಷ ವರದಿ
  or "ಪ್ರಾಯೋಜಿತ ಸುದ್ದಿ", so nobody mistakes an ad for a story.
* No sponsor's name, logo, bumper or footer appears inside our news: not in
  the three formats, not in a WhatsApp news digest or bulletin, not on a
  breaking card. A sponsor gets its own, labelled post.

## 2 · Every paid piece says so

* The label is on the work, up front: on the first frame or card, readable
  at phone size, in the first seconds of a video — not only in the caption
  or the last hashtag (ASCI Code and its guidelines for influencer
  advertising in digital media).
* The platform's own tool is used as well: Meta's paid-partnership label on
  Instagram and Facebook; "includes paid promotion" on YouTube.

## 3 · Sponsors in the news

When a story names a current or recent sponsor, the editor is told before it
is published (`scripts/business.py conflicts`). The editor either says so
on the story, or has it judged by someone with no stake in the deal. Being a
sponsor buys neither coverage nor silence.

## 4 · What we do not advertise

* Online money games and betting — Promotion and Regulation of Online
  Gaming Act, 2025.
* Tobacco — COTPA, 2003; liquor, and products that stand in for it.
* Remedies claiming to cure the diseases listed in the Drugs and Magic
  Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954.

We read claims carefully, and may ask for documents, for: hospitals and
health products, finance, chit funds, loans and insurance, education ("100%
results", "guaranteed job"), real estate (RERA number), cryptocurrency,
religious and government advertising.

## 5 · Political advertising

A political advertisement by a party or a candidate runs only with its
Media Certification and Monitoring Committee (MCMC) pre-certificate number,
and is labelled as an advertisement. Paid news — payment for coverage
presented as news — is something we never sell, and during an election it
is an electoral offence (Election Commission of India; Press Council of
India norms).

## 6 · Who decides

A person at ಊರ್ಮನಿ ಸುದ್ದಿ clears every deal before it is published. Our
assistants may draft and check; they do not accept, sign or publish
advertising.

Questions or complaints about an advertisement go to the Grievance Officer
named on every closing slide (IT Rules 2021, Part III).
